The '''Apple''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}''', the '''{{IconSAP|Puppy Pack}}''' and the '''{{IconSAP|Golden Pack}}'''. When a pet is fed an Apple, it receives +1 attack and +1 health. The {{IconSAP|Pear}}, {{IconSAP|Lasagna}} and {{IconSAP|Chicken Leg}} are generally considered to be direct upgrades to it.

The {{IconSAP|Mouse}} and {{IconSAP|Worm}} will stock an Apple when sold/at the start of turn, at level 2 they will stock a {{IconSAP|Better Apple|nolink=yes}} and at level 3 they will stock a {{IconSAP|Best Apple|nolink=yes}} that give +2/+2 and +3/+3 respectively.

Sleeping Pill:Make one pet faint. Always on sale!
The '''Sleeping Pill''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 2}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}''' and the '''{{IconSAP|Puppy Pack}}'''. When a [[Pets|pet]] is fed a Sleeping Pill, they're knocked out and are permanently removed from the team. However, this will also activate their '''[[Faint (Trigger)|Faint]]''' abilities.

Sleeping Pill costs 1 gold, instead of the usual 3 for all other Foods.

Sleeping Pill does not trigger '''Eats food''' abilities.

Mushroom:Give one pet the Mushroom [[Food Perks|perk]]. Come back as a 1/1 after fainting.
'''Mushroom''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 6}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Feeding a [[Pets|pet]] a Mushroom gives that pet the Mushroom [[Food Perks|perk]]. When a pet with the Mushroom perk faints, it summons a 1/1 copy of itself at the same level.

Summoned copies are considered "new" pets, with almost no memory of their old selves before they faint. For example if a {{IconSAP|Parrot}} faints and comes back, it will not keep the copied abilities. However, Mushroom ''does'' remember the amount of ability triggers the pet has spent.

The '''Pizza''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 6}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Buying a Pizza gives two random pets +2 attack and +2 health permanently. The Pizza can be considered as a direct upgrade to the {{IconSAP|Salad Bowl}}, as it gives twice the stats of it.
