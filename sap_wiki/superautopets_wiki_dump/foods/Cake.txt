''This page details information about the incremental sell value perk. For other cake-related foods, see {{IconSAP|Chocolate Cake}} or {{IconSAP|Eat-Me Cake}}.''


The '''Cake''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 3}} [[Food]], available for free in '''{{IconSAP|Custom Packs}}'''. Feeding a pet the Cake will give them the Cake [[Perk|perk]]. A pet with the Cake perk will increase its sell value by 1 {{IconSAP|gold}} at the end of the turn.

==Strategy==
With Cake's availability in the early game, it works well on strong pets you plan on selling later. For example, a Level 2 {{IconSAP|Cricket}} at 3/5 can help your team early in the game but will inevitably need to be sold for a stronger replacement. With Cake, you can use the Cricket as an investment so that a burst of gold is earned once it's finally sold.

Beyond '''Turtle Pack''', the sell value buffs synergize with specific pet abilities found in '''{{IconSAP|Custom Packs}}'''. For each gained sell value, {{IconSAP|Trout}} earns more health when that friend is sold. Additionally, holding onto high sell value pets allows {{IconSAP|Pacific Fanfish}} to gain more buffs at the start of battles.

==Trivia==
