'''Canned Food''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 4}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}''' and the '''{{IconSAP|Puppy Pack}}'''. When bought, instead of giving stats to pets currently on the team, it gives permanent +1 {{IconSAP|attack|nolink=yes}} and +1 {{IconSAP|health|nolink=yes}} to all current ''and future'' shop pets.

== Strategy ==
Canned Food, or simply known as "Can", is a food that supports pivoting. It's an {{IconSAP|Apple}} for any pet that you might put on the team later, even ones that haven't shown up yet such as tier 5 and 6 pets, giving them a head start in scaling. Another way to conceptualize its strength is if the entire team will eventually be replaced by high tier pets, then each Can is 3 gold for +5/+5. It pairs well with pivot pets such as {{IconSAP|Turkey}}, {{IconSAP|Lobster}}, and {{IconSAP|Chicken}}.

Since the buffs can accumulate, buying more Cans will make the shop bigger and bigger. The effect works with {{IconSAP|Cat}}'s ability, making Cat Can a hard to pull off, but extremely rewarding strategy that can max out the stats of all shop pets.

==Trivia==
*The Canned Food is the first food to affect Shop pets.
