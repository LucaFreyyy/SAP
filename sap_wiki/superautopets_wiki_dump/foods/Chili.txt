'''Chili''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 5}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Feeding a [[Pets|pet]] a Chili will give that pet the Chili [[Food Perks|perk]]. Every time a pet with Chili perk attacks, it will deal an additional 5 damage to the pet in second space of the enemy's team. If there are no enemies in that space, Chili will not deal damage.

== Strategy ==
Chili acts as chip damage to pets behind the main target. It is useful for countering second position supporters such as {{IconSAP|Snake}} or {{IconSAP|Tiger}}, breaking backline {{IconSAP|Melon}} perks before they become a threat once they're in front, and especially useful against summoners such as {{IconSAP|Rooster}} or {{IconSAP|Fly}} to sweep all the high attack but low health tokens.

When paired with {{IconSAP|Panther}}, Chili becomes even scarier with 2x/3x/4x damage to the second enemy. A level 3 Panther with Chili will deal ''20 damage'' to the second enemy.
