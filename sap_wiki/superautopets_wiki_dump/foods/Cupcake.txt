The '''Cupcake''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 2}} [[Food]].  When a [[Pets|pet]] is fed a Cupcake, it gets +3/+3 until the end of battle.

== Strategy ==
Using the cupcake may be worth it in the very early-game, where most pets don't have high stats, to gain a significant edge. Later on however, where scalers become more prevalent, the Cupcake becomes comparatively inefficient since you can most likely buff your pets permanently and at a larger degree than the cupcake.
