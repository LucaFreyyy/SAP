The '''Honey''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} [[Food]], available in  the'''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Feeding a [[Pets|pet]] Honey will give it the Honey [[Food Perks|perk]]. When a pet with Honey perk faints, it summons a level 1 {{IconSAP|Bee}} in its place.

== Strategy ==
The Honey can be placed on pets in front of those who have the Friend ahead attacks ability trigger such as {{IconSAP|Snake}} and {{IconSAP|Kangaroo}} or in teams which benefit from [[Summon Pets]] with abilities such as {{IconSAP|Horse|name=Horse's}} and {{IconSAP|Turkey|name=Turkey's}}. It can also be used in builds with certain [[Faint Pets]] that have abilities like that of the {{IconSAP|Shark|name=Shark's}}.

Whilst the Honey is a somewhat potent food early game, it loses its value quite harshly later on and thus should be swapped for other [[Food Perks]] such as the {{IconSAP|Melon|name=Melon perk}} or the {{IconSAP|Steak|name=Steak perk}}.

Honey can be used as a tiebreaker, as the Bee will spawn after the last 2 Pets fought, allowing you to win.

It is a good early game food, but performs disadvantageously against pets that have the [[Knock Out (Trigger)|Knock Out]] or [[Hurt (Trigger)|Hurt abilities]] (e.g. {{IconSAP|Hippo}}, {{IconSAP|Blowfish}}) as it will give them a safe way to activate their abilities.
