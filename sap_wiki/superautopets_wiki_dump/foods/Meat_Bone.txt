The '''Meat Bone''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 2}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Feeding a pet a Meat Bone will give them the Meat Bone [[Food Perks|perk]]. A pet with the Meat Bone perk attacks with +3 extra damage. 

This attack bonus does not affect abilities that are dependent on attack: Like {{IconSAP|Dodo}} or {{IconSAP|Leopard}}.
