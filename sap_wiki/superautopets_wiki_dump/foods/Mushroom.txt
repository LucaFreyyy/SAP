'''Mushroom''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 6}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Feeding a [[Pets|pet]] a Mushroom gives that pet the Mushroom [[Food Perks|perk]]. When a pet with the Mushroom perk faints, it summons a 1/1 copy of itself at the same level.

Summoned copies are considered "new" pets, with almost no memory of their old selves before they faint. For example if a {{IconSAP|Parrot}} faints and comes back, it will not keep the copied abilities. However, Mushroom ''does'' remember the amount of ability triggers the pet has spent.

== Strategy ==
Mushroom's biggest strength is giving pets an additional, guaranteed chance to trigger their abilities. Some examples include the {{IconSAP|Elephant}}, {{IconSAP|Turtle}}, {{IconSAP|Deer}}, {{IconSAP|Mammoth}}. It combines well with {{IconSAP|Scorpion}}, enabling it to break {{IconSAP|Melon}} on its first attack, then respawn with {{IconSAP|Peanut}} and knock out the exposed enemy.

It can also be used as a defensive perk for support pets that don't care much about their own stats, such as {{IconSAP|Turkey}}, {{IconSAP|Tiger}}, {{IconSAP|Snake}}. Mushroom on these pets is as strong if not stronger than {{IconSAP|Bread}} and {{IconSAP|Melon}}, since the pet is guaranteed to survive any Start of battle snipers.

==Trivia==
