The '''Pear''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 4}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Feeding a [[Pets|Pet]] a Pear increases its stats by +2 attack and +2 health permanently. 

Essentially, it's a better {{IconSAP|Apple}} but a worse {{IconSAP|Lasagna}}/{{IconSAP|Chicken Leg}}.

==Strategy==
There isn't a lot to be said, feed it to pets when appropriate and generally try to avoid feeding it to pets you don't intend to keep for long. The {{IconSAP|Seal}} and {{IconSAP|Rabbit}} create more stats from feeding pets food and Pears are a solid option to trigger those effects. The Pear's stats can also be multiplied by a {{IconSAP|Cat}}.
