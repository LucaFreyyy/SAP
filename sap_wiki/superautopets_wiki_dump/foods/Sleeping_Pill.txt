The '''Sleeping Pill''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 2}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}''' and the '''{{IconSAP|Puppy Pack}}'''. When a [[Pets|pet]] is fed a Sleeping Pill, they're knocked out and are permanently removed from the team. However, this will also activate their '''[[Faint (Trigger)|Faint]]''' abilities.

Sleeping Pill costs 1 gold, instead of the usual 3 for all other Foods.

Sleeping Pill does not trigger '''Eats food''' abilities.

==Strategy==
Sleeping Pill is a gold efficient food and that's used to extract values out of Faint abilities, often means gaining extra stats, higher tier pets and perks, or that might be otherwise inaccessible.

Most [[Faint Pets]] or food synergize with Pill. Here are some of the most prominent examples:

* '''Stats boosts''': {{IconSAP|Ant}}, {{IconSAP|Flamingo}}, {{IconSAP|Mammoth}} all give nice stat boosts when they faint. At tier 2, Flamingo Pill is 4 gold for a targeted {{IconSAP|Salad Bowl}} (a tier 3 food).
* '''Higher tier pets''': {{IconSAP|Spider}}, {{IconSAP|Beluga Sturgeon}}, and {{IconSAP|Eagle}} will all summon a pet from 1 tier higher when they faint.
* '''Higher tier perks''': {{IconSAP|Melon}} is a tier 6 perk that can be obtained as early as ''turn 3'' by pilling something in front of an {{IconSAP|Ox}}, or a bit later by pilling a {{IconSAP|Turtle}}.
* '''Achievement hunting''': Pilling summoners like {{IconSAP|Sheep}}, {{IconSAP|Deer}}, or {{IconSAP|Rooster}} will summon their tokens during shop phase, leading to their secret achievements and ribbons.
