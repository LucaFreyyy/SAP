The '''Camel''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 3}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. When the Camel [[Hurt (Trigger)|is hurt]], it gives a buff to the nearest friend behind it. Levelling up the Camel increases the size of the buff given. 


==Strategy==
The Camel is best when paired with animals that cause damage to the player's own team, like {{IconSAP|Elephant}} and {{IconSAP|Rat}} (via {{IconSAP|Dirty Rat}}) that can do small amounts of damage. The stats it offers during battle when synergizing with them can quickly overwhelm an opponent's board through raw stats.

Camel can look to buff a unit that synergizes with the during-battle stats to sweep, such as {{IconSAP|Ox}} with its {{IconSAP|Melon}}.

==Trivia==
* The Camel's sprite is a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bactrian_camel Bactrian], or a two-humped camel, named after a historical region in Central Asia. This is as opposed to the single-humped camel, known as a {{IconSAP|Dromedary}}.
** Unlike the Dromedary, which lives in the hot deserts of Arabia and North Africa, the Bactrian lives in cold steppes and mountains, and is therefore much fuzzier.
** The Camel’s sprite has long eyelashes because real camels do, to protect their eyes from the harsh sunlight of the desert.
