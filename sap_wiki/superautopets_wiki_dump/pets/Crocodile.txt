The '''Crocodile''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 5}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. At the start of each battle, the Crocodile deals 8 {{IconSAP|damage|nolink=yes}} to the Pet at the back of the enemy team. Levelling up the Crocodile will increase the amount of times it deals 8 damage to the backmost enemy pet.

==Strategy==
Crocodile is a simple-yet-effective anti-backline pet. Enemies in backmost positions tend to have low stats and abilities that will become troublesome during battle such as {{IconSAP|Shark}}, {{IconSAP|Turkey}}, and {{IconSAP|Fly}}. In any cases, Crocodile will deal a substantial amount of damage to them, then repeat the snipe at higher levels to either break through their defensive perks to ensure a knockout, or if they have already been knocked out, damage the next enemies in the back.

While Crocodile ability stands on its own with no obvious combos, it can be very effective when paired with other '''Start of battle''' snipers and repeaters such as {{IconSAP|Dolphin}}, {{IconSAP|Parrot}}, other Crocodiles, and {{IconSAP|Tiger}}. Crocodile is exceptionally powerful against most summon teams, and more snipes means knocking out more pets to really crush these builds.
