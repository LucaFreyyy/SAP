The '''Dog''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 3}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. Whenever a friend is summoned, the Dog will gain a stat buff. However, the stats will only last until end of battle. Levelling up the Dog will increase the amount of stats gained for each friend summoned.  

==Strategy ==
The Dog has mediocre stats. It can be a heavy hitter for a few turns early on if you're buying and selling lots of pets, but tends to fall off quickly unless supported by a summoning team featuring units like the {{IconSAP|Sheep}} and the {{IconSAP|Fly}}. Even then, its ability is weak compared to that of pets like the {{IconSAP|Hippo}} and the {{IconSAP|Kangaroo}}. The dog pairs very well with the {{IconSAP|Dragon}} in the {{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}, but gets overshadowed by other buy/sell pets in customs like the {{IconSAP|Buffalo}}.
