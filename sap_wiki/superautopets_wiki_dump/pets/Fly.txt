The '''Fly''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 6}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. When a friend faints, it will summon a {{IconSAP|Zombie Fly}} where that pet fainted. This ability can only activate 3 times per turn. Levelling up the Fly increases the stats and level of the summoned Zombie Fly

Fly's ability will not trigger until there are spaces for the Zombie Flies to spawn in. When used with other Faint abilities such as {{IconSAP|Rooster}} or {{IconSAP|Deer}}, it will "wait" until the tokens have been cleared before summoning Zombie Flies.

When a Zombie Fly faints, it will not trigger Fly's ability.

==Strategy==
Fly is a good value buy since it's almost guaranteed to give 3 summons, something that other summoners can accidentally waste by running out of space. It can be thought of as 3 gold for a 16/16, and can sometimes be used just for the pure stats it can provide.

Fly pairs well with summoning pets such as {{IconSAP|Turkey}} as well as {{IconSAP|Shark}}, since one Fly is equal to 4 faint triggers in battle. It also combos nicely with {{IconSAP|Snake}}: whatever that's in front of Snake will turn into a Zombie Fly after fainting and give Snake an extra snipe. Fly tends to be positioned at the last 2 spaces to make sure enough faints occur for it to use up all of its triggers, which often makes it vulnerable to {{IconSAP|Crocodile}}.

===Custom packs===
A niche synergy the Fly has is with Faint Scaling teams ({{IconSAP|Shark}}, {{IconSAP|Praying Mantis}} and {{IconSAP|Spinosaurus}}), in which it will summon a Zombie Fly that can be sold for 1, 2 or 3 gold (depending on level) everytime a friendly pet faints in the shop from a {{IconSAP|Sleeping Pill}} or the Praying Mantis' ability.
