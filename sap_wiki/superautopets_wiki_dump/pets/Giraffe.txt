The '''Giraffe''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 3}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}''' . At the start of every turn, the Giraffe will buff the stats of the friend(s) ahead of it. Levelling up the Giraffe will increase the amount of friends it can target with its ability. 

==Strategy==
The Giraffe is respectable scaling for its tier. At Tier 3, you can look to place your giraffe behind a pet you will keep in the future battles, such as an {{IconSAP|Ox}} or a {{IconSAP|Badger}}.

When levelled up, you can buff animals more than one space ahead of it, allowing you to buff animals that require to be placed together without ruining their synergy, {{IconSAP|Camel}} and {{IconSAP|Elephant}}.

Starting from Tier 5 and higher, Giraffe's scaling is very easily outclassed by other scaling pets such as {{IconSAP|Penguin}} or {{IconSAP|Monkey}}. One should opt to a stronger scaling option as soon as possible.
