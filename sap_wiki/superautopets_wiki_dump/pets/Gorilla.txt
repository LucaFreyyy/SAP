The '''Gorilla''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 6}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. When the Gorilla [[Hurt (Trigger)|is hurt]], it will gain the {{IconSAP|Coconut}} [[Food Perks|perk]], which completely blocks a single instance of damage. However, this ability only triggers a limited number of times per battle. Levelling up the Gorilla increases the amount of times the Coconut perk can be gained.


==Strategy==
With the Custom and Weekly Packs, the Gorilla can be paired with a {{IconSAP|Pepper}}, meaning that it will always trigger its ability, gaining Coconut, guaranteeing that it will be able to attack at least 3 times without dying. This is opposed to giving it {{IconSAP|Melon}}, which runs the risk of the Gorilla not taking damage or taking enough damage that it dies in one hit ({{IconSAP|Salt}}, {{IconSAP|Fortune Cookie}} or {{IconSAP|Sword Fish}}) and therefore not gaining Coconut safely.

If paired with a {{IconSAP|Leech}}, the Gorilla can get permanent Coconut, and still have triggers in the battle.

One fun yet impractical setup is to put a {{IconSAP|Parrot}} behind the Gorilla, and a Leech behind the Parrot. The Parrot will gain permanent Coconut, regardless of if the Leech triggers before the Parrot.

==Trivia==

*Using a Leech, Parrot, Gorilla, and a couple of [[Seagull|Seagulls]], you can get permanent Coconut perk on any pet you want, detailed in this [https://www.reddit.com/r/superautopets/comments/vq4ij1/full_team_of_5050s_with_coconut_armor_in_shop/ reddit post].
