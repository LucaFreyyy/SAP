The '''Horse''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Whenever a friendly pet fills an empty slot, either by buying them or via an ability, Horse will give them a small amount of attack until next turn. Leveling up the Horse increases the amount of attack it gives to summoned friends.

Horse has a hidden interaction: It can be merged with {{IconSAP|Eagle}} to transform into a 5/5 level 1 {{IconSAP|Hippogriff}}.

==Strategy==
Horse strengthens any pet newly added to the team, improves the battle capabilities of summoners such as {{IconSAP|Cricket}}, {{IconSAP|Honey}} perk, {{IconSAP|Spider}}, {{IconSAP|Sheep}}, and can easily dominate the first few turns. Keeping a Horse on the team also makes it easier to pivot later; you don't lose too much stats when selling existing pets since the replacement will receive some attack from Horse, albeit only for one turn.

If you're planning to buy a Horse, it should be bought before other pets so it can give them extra attack for the next battle. When paired with summoners, it should be put behind them to buff the summoned pets in battle. Although, with 1 health at base stats, Horse is very vulnerable to snipers such as {{IconSAP|Mosquito}} and {{IconSAP|Dolphin}} that will knock it out before it can contribute anything to the battle.

For summon teams, you can find a direct upgrade at tier 5: {{IconSAP|Turkey}}, which not only gives more stats but also permanently instead of temporarily. For other scaling focused teams, Horse's ability quickly becomes useless with no targets to give buffs to, and should be replaced ASAP.
