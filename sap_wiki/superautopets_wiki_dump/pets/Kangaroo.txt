The '''Kangaroo''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 2}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. When the friend ahead of the Kangaroo attacks, the Kangaroo gains a buff to its stats. Leveling up the Kangaroo increases the buff it receives each time its ability is triggered. 

==Strategy==
The Kangaroo is best placed behind a beefy friend, such as an '''{{IconSAP|Ox|image=Ox.png}}''' (when it has its Melon ability active) or a {{IconSAP|Peacock|image=Peacock.png}} that can tank many hits to ensure that your Kangaroo will be a formidable pet that will go up next.

Putting the Kangaroo behind a summoning pet such as a {{IconSAP|Spider|image=Spider.png}} or a {{IconSAP|Sheep|image=Sheep.png}} is also very effective, as it guarantees two procs for the Kangaroo, one proc for the main pet and the other for the pet it summons.
