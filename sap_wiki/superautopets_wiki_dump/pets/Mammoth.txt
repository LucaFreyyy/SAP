The '''Mammoth''' is a [[Tier 6]] [[Pets|Pet]] in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. When the Mammoth faints, it gives all of its friends an increased stat boost. Levelling up the Mammoth will increase the stat boost it provides to all friends.

==Strategy==

Mammoth is an incredibly powerful form of in-battle scaling, often capable of catching up and beating opposing scaling boards in {{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}} before they get too far out of reach. Its large starting base stats and strong utility make it a great buff target, and one of the best things to level up into on turn 9. 

Pilling Mammoth should be a fairly rare play unless you have {{IconSAP|Shark}}, since you often lose a lot of stats on the mammoth itself to make that play. Cycling {{IconSAP|Mushroom}} and {{IconSAP|Sleeping Pill}} can be viable even without shark, especially if you have some form of gold generation or need the stats to survive start of battle snipes!

Mammoth synergizes well with pets and perks that can multiply its effect. With these multipliers you can often gain as many stats as you'd be getting from using a scaler like {{IconSAP|Cat}} or {{IconSAP|Dragon}}. In Turtle Pack, these multipliers include:

- {{IconSAP|Mushroom}} - Doesn't take up any extra board space to double the Mammoth's effect! Can be exploited to get additional triggers on hurt pets or snake.

- {{IconSAP|Whale}} - Fastest way to get Mammoth buffs, arguably Whale's best partner! 

- {{IconSAP|Tiger}} - Unlike Whale and Parrot, Tiger can fully double the effect of a Mushroom'd Mammoth. At level 2 Tiger-Mammoth can potentially give +16/16 to all pets, but make sure to keep the Tiger safe from snipes!

- {{IconSAP|Parrot}} - Probably Mammoth's weakest and slowest multiplier, but can be a great option if you have a level 2 Parrot already! 



==Trivia==

Mammoth and {{IconSAP|Dodo}} are only the Ice Age pets in the {{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}.
