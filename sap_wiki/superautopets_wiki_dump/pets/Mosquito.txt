The '''Mosquito''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. It has subpar stats, and at the start of battle, the Mosquito will deal 1 damage to a random enemy. Levelling up the Mosquito will increase the amount of enemies its ability will target.


==Strategy==
The Mosquito feels mediocre, and only takable if you have atleast 2 or 3 copies of it in the shop during your first turn. It provides the same amount of tempo (value during the turn it is played) as {{IconSAP|Duck}}, {{IconSAP|Beaver}}, and {{IconSAP|Fish}}, except that unlike them, the Mosquito provides no benefit mid-game, and has a possible negative effect of it being able hit Hurt ability pets like {{IconSAP|Peacock}}, more often in versus.

The Mosquito can pop {{IconSAP|Melon}} and harm other cases of perks that reduce damage a limited amount of times. While that is true, it happens lategame. You run the risk of putting it in mid-game, with it being underpowered there. Also, there's better means of popping Melons, such as {{IconSAP|Rhino}}, or simply putting a weak unit up-front.
