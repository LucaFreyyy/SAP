The '''Ox''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 3}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. When the nearest friend ahead of Ox faints, Ox will gain {{IconSAP|Melon}} [[Food Perks|Food perk]] and +1 attack. Leveling up Ox will increase the amount of times its ability can trigger per turn.

==Strategy==
Ox is one of the earliest pets in the game with access to a formidable ''tier 6'' perk. Melon is extremely powerful in all stages of the game. In early turns, since almost nothing can deal more than 20 damage, Ox is essentially invincible for one attack. It can also be thought of as having immunity against snipers breaking the Melon, since as long as it's not in front-most position, it will refresh its own Melon by the time it gets to attack.

It's a good target to focus stats on since the ability will remain useful until the end of the game. It synergies very well with all '''Faint''' pets such as {{IconSAP|Ant}}, {{IconSAP|Flamingo}}, {{IconSAP|Spider}}, and so on. {{IconSAP|Sleeping Pill|name=Pilling}} any of these pets in front of Ox during shop phase will give it permanent Melon and a bonus +1 attack. Pilling a {{IconSAP|Turtle}} in front of Ox while it already has Melon will still give +1 attack while passing a new Melon over to another friend behind. Pill synergies also make it an amazing teammate with {{IconSAP|Shark}} later on.

At the same tier, Ox can combo with a front {{IconSAP|Badger}}. When Badger faints, it will pop the Melon, then Ox will immediately regain the Melon. Though this is rather risky if anything breaks the Melon beforehand and leaves Ox vulnerable to Badger's snipe.
