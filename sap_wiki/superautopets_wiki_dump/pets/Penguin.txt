The '''Penguin''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 4}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. At the start of every turn, the Penguin will give a stat buff to two random level 2 or 3 friends. Leveling up the Penguin will increase the amount of stats that are given. 

==Strategy==
The Penguin is a versatile wide scaler, with its restrictions being very relaxed compared to similar friendly scalers from other packs such as {{IconSAP|Siberian Husky}} or the {{IconSAP|Poodle}}, and without positioning constraints like the ones imposed by {{IconSAP|Giraffe}} or {{IconSAP|Monkey}}.
