'''Rabbit''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 3}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. When a friendly pet eats any [[Food]], including Rabbit itself, Rabbit will give that pet additional health, up to three times per turn. Leveling up Rabbit increases the amount of health given.

==Strategy==
Rabbit gives extra health every time pets on the team receive foods and perks. At level 1, it's effectively multiplying the stats affect of all foods: 2x effect for {{IconSAP|Bread Crumbs}}, 1.5x for {{IconSAP|Apple}} and {{IconSAP|Salad Bowl}}, 1.25x for {{IconSAP|Pear}}, and so on. Multiple Rabbits can be used to give a more and more health.

Being a pure health scaler, it synergizes well with Hurt pets such as {{IconSAP|Peacock}} and {{IconSAP|Camel}} that love to be hit multiple times, but can also work for just building up a beefy tank that will stay until the end of the game. It also has synergies with {{IconSAP|Ox}}, {{IconSAP|Turtle}}, and {{IconSAP|Scorpion}} to give them extra health when they gain/give perks.

Just due to how efficient Rabbit is, it can easily snowball a pet into a 10/20 as early as turn 5, and can quickly be swapped out for something else once you've got yourself enough health scaling. In Turtle pack, a 20/40 will get to 50 health before you know it, but it will take a bit longer until it can reach 50 attack. You'll also be able to find an upgrade to Rabbit at tier 6: {{IconSAP|Cat}}.
