The '''Rat''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 2}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. When the Rat faints, it summons a level 1 {{IconSAP|Dirty Rat}} at the front of ''the enemy's team''. Leveling up the Rat will increase the amount of Dirty Rats summoned.

==Strategy==
Rat will summon pets for the enemy, which is almost always a detriment. It's a risky buy that is often only used for its high stats in early turns. You should not overvalue its potential synergies with other pets. Never put Rat in front of {{IconSAP|Ox}}. And '''never''' put Rat in the last position.

With all that said, since Dirty Rats are weak and can be easily knocked out, they can be used to give additional triggers to certain types of ability: '''Hurt''' pets ({{IconSAP|Peacock}}, {{IconSAP|Camel}}), '''Knock out''' pets ({{IconSAP|Hippo}}, {{IconSAP|Rhino}}), and '''After attack''' pets ({{IconSAP|Elephant}}). By putting Rat directly in front of pets with these abilities, it will summon Dirty Rats for the opponent which when attacked will trigger the ability at the cost of only 1 damage per Dirty Rat. These synergies can be further amplified with supporters like {{IconSAP|Kangaroo}}, {{IconSAP|Snake}}, or {{IconSAP|Tiger}} behind the pet that will be facing the Dirty Rats.

Another use for Rat is body blocking the enemy's summons. For example, when Rat trades with a {{IconSAP|Deer}} and they knock each other out, if the Rat has higher attack, the Dirty Rats will be summoned first, blocking the {{IconSAP|Bus}} from being summoned. Though this is rather niche and rarely happens on purpose.

To avoid less or no Dirty Rats being spawned, some enemies must first be knocked out to create space. This often means Rat has to be scaled and act as a tank or else it will waste the ability. Although, not triggering the ability might actually be a positive.
