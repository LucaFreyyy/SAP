The '''Shark''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 5}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. Each time one of its friends faints, the Shark gains a moderate amount of stats. Leveling up the Shark will increase the amount of stats it gains.  

==Strategy==
Shark is a pet that gets stronger as the battle progresses, similar to {{IconSAP|Kangaroo}} or {{IconSAP|Camel}} combos from earlier tiers. A back-most Shark will have at minimum 4 faint triggers and gain +8/+8, so it can already be a 10/10 on the turn it's bought. Shark fits nicely in summon teams that can feed it with even more faint triggers. It also gets some extra benefits from {{IconSAP|Sleeping Pill|name=Pilling}} pets during shop phases, and will be very happy to see {{IconSAP|Turtle}} Pill and {{IconSAP|Mammoth}} Pill. 

Shark is a good counter to {{IconSAP|Skunk}}, it can either tank the health reduction then gain them back during battle, or dodge the Skunk completely due to starting out at lower health. However since it's usually placed behind and has low base stats, it's very susceptible to {{IconSAP|Crocodile}} as well as other snipers.
