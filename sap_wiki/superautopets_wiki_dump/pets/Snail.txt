'''Snail''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 2}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. At the end of turn, if the player lost the last battle, Snail will give the three nearest friends ahead {{IconSAP|attack|nolink=yes}}. Leveling up Snail increases the amount of attack it gives.

Snail's ability will only trigger if the player ''lost'' the last battle, not if it was a draw. This is indicated by a sparkling effect on the Snail while it's in the shop.

If Snail is bought on turn 1, the "last battle" will be considered a draw.

==Strategy==
Snail can be thought of as compensation for losing, or as a passive scaler with a condition. If kept around, Snail can give its buffs for a maximum of 5 times as you progress through the game. It should be placed in the last 2 positions so as to not waste the buffs.

If you lose on turn 2, due to the life regain on turn 3, Snail can trigger the buff without costing a life. Depending on how much you value Snail's buff, you can try to sabotage your team on turn 2 to guarantee Snail trigger.
