The '''Squirrel''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 4}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. At the start of each turn, Squirrel will reduce the {{IconSAP|gold}} cost of all [[Foods]] that are currently in the shop. Leveling up the Squirrel will further reduce the costs of the foods.

==Strategy==
Squirrel relies on investing in freezing certain foods to purchase them at a discounted price on future turns. Most Foods at {{IconSAP|Tier 4}} and above are worth freezing, with current team synergy having a heavy hand in what should be prioritised. {{IconSAP|Chocolate}} will almost always be valuable to any team and is often worth making use of the Squirrel's ability to purchase more easily.

Squirrel has good synergy with most [[Food Pets]], specifically the Pets that are able to increase the amount of stats gained from purchasing foods like {{IconSAP|Rabbit}}, {{IconSAP|Seal}} and {{IconSAP|Rabbit}}. These pets further increase the value of reducing the cost of shop Foods due to the extra stats they are able to give per gold spent.

The Squirrel also synergises somewhat effectively with {{IconSAP|Worm}}. If the Squirrel has less attack than the Worm then the Worm will stock its 2-gold {{IconSAP|Apple}} and then the Squirrel will discount it in addition to the other shop foods. This is especially effective with early Rabbit(s) and in '''{{IconSAP|Custom Packs}}''' with {{IconSAP|Jerboa}} teams.

At level 1, it affects the {{IconSAP|Sleeping Pill}}, At level 2, it affects the Worm's {{IconSAP|Apple}}, At level 3, it is easy since all food are now 0 gold. However, When you roll the shop, the cost of all food will become normal.

==Trivia==
Both sprites of the Squirrel is holding an Acorn.

The Squirrel pet is one of three pets based on a squirrel, the others being the {{IconSAP|Albino Squirrel}} and the {{IconSAP|Giant Squirrel}}.
