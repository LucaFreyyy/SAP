The '''Tiger''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 6}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. While in battle, whenever the nearest pet ahead of the Tiger triggers their ability, they will immediately trigger it again but this time at the same level as the Tiger.

That bad boy works with '''bold texts''', say, '''Hurt''', '''Faint''', '''Friend summoned''', yeah you know the deal. It doesn't work with '''End Turn''' or '''Start of turn''' abilities like {{IconSAP|Giraffe}},{{IconSAP|Monkey}}, or, well, another Tiger.

Friends repeating their abilities won't deplete additional triggers. For example: Each time a friend faints, {{IconSAP|Fly}} would summon 2 {{IconSAP|Zombie Fly|name=Zombie Flies}} but only lose 1 trigger.

==Strategy==
Tiger is a unique supporter that upgrades combat abilities, often making that pet twice as strong. For example, putting a Tiger behind {{IconSAP|Snake}} will make the Snake shoots twice each time it's triggered.

At the moment (08/01/2026), {{IconSAP|Tiger}} has been changed for to deplete triggers, so, you will get faster activations, but they won't last too much!


'''{{IconSAP|Tiger}}''' + '''{{IconSAP|Rhino}}''' is a great combo!, it can wipe out pets with a single attack!. '''

{{ARTICLEPAGENAME}}

Tiger's ability is a bit similar to {{IconSAP|Parrot}}. But while they both share a similar audience, they each have their own strengths, use cases, and pets that they work better on. For example, while both {{IconSAP|Tiger}} {{IconSAP|Turkey}} and {{IconSAP|Parrot}} {{IconSAP|Turkey}} serve the same purpose for summon teams, Parrot can be found much earlier and would be higher level than Tiger. An example would be '''Hurt''' combos like with {{IconSAP|Camel}} or {{IconSAP|Blowfish}}, Tiger will make these combos up to twice as powerful, while Parrot cannot.

Being one of the pets with the most synergies in the entire game, there's a whole lot of tiger combos. It's usually put behind pets with strong combat abilities, but there are plenty of different ways to utilize Tiger. Get creative!

Below is some unique interactions that you should know: 

* Tiger will always repeat the ability of whatever that's currently in front, even when it moves out of its original position. This is useful for Custom packs utilizing pets like {{IconSAP|Panda}}, {{IconSAP|Sword Fish}}, or packs with {{IconSAP|Onion}}.
* It doesn't necessarily ALWAYS make the friend ahead stronger. Since the ability is ''repeated'' at the same level as the Tiger, for certain abilities like {{IconSAP|Crab}} or {{IconSAP|Butterfly}}, a low level Tiger will make them perform their copy abilities again at lower level, and end up copying less stats.

Formerly, if you used tiger on something like {{IconSAP|Egyptian Vulture}}, it could potentially repeat all 3 triggers of the Egyptian Vulture, even at level 1.
