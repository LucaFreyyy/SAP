The '''Turtle''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 4}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}'''. When Turtle faints, it gives the nearest friend behind it {{IconSAP|Melon}} [[Food Perks|perk]], overriding other [[Food Perks|Food Perk]] or [[Ailments|Ailment]] they had previously. When leveled up, Turtle will give Melon perk to more friends behind.

==Strategy==
Turtle is most commonly bought and {{IconSAP|Sleeping Pill|name=Pilled}} to gain early access to Melon. When kept on the team, it combos best with scaling teams that benefit more from the protection of Melon than the attack stats from {{IconSAP|Steak}} or synergies with {{IconSAP|Mushroom}}. It's commonly put directly ahead of the pet with the highest attack/health in the team, which usually means it'll be the front-most pet while the strongest pet is in the second position. This accomplishes 3 things:

#'''Refreshing the team's Melon''': Snipers like {{IconSAP|Mosquito}}, {{IconSAP|Blowfish}}, {{IconSAP|Leopard}} or Ailment pets from other packs like {{IconSAP|Bat}} or {{IconSAP|Toad}} can strip the team off Melons. Turtle can counter this by giving Melon afterward.
#'''Breaking the enemy's Melon''': A common strategy is having a strong pet in front with a defensive food perk. If that perk was Melon, Turtle will pop it and allows our strong pet now with Melon to face against one without Melon.
#'''Countering {{IconSAP|Scorpion}}''': Similarly, Scorpion is usually in front and will be knocked out by our Turtle.

Turtle also "synergizes" with Melon itself: It will skip over friends that already have Melons, so putting more Melons on your team simply ensure they will always have protections both at Start of battle and during battle.

===Custom packs===
By combining all of this with a pet like the {{IconSAP|Elephant Seal}} or the {{IconSAP|Axolotl}}, you can gain massive amounts of stats whenever your turtle faints. Another strategy would be to combine the Turtle with {{IconSAP|Microbe}} or {{IconSAP|Pandoras Box}} from {{IconSAP|Bad Dog}}. Pandoras Box will give your whole team perks/ailments, but none of them will matter because the Turtle will replace all of those bad perks/ailments with {{IconSAP|Melon}}.
