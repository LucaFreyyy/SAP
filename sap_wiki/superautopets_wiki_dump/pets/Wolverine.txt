The '''Wolverine''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 6}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. The Wolverine removes health from all enemies when enough friends are hurt. Leveling the Wolverine up increases the health removed. Removing health is distinct from damage, will not trigger hurt abilities or be reduced by garlic, and cannot remove health past 1.

==Strategy==
Wolverine synergizes extremely well with [[Elephant]], as each Elephant trigger will count towards the Wolverine countdown. It can also work with summon builds, as there are many pets to be hurt to count for the countdown.
