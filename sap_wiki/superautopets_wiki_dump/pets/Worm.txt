The '''Worm''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 2}} [[Pets|Pet]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack|image=Turtle Icon.png}}'''. At the start of turn the Worm will stock the food shop with a 2-gold {{IconSAP|Apple}}. Levelling up the Worm increases the stats given by the stocked Apple.

==Strategy==
Worm's ability to stock apples makes it great synergy with food-related scaling pets, such as {{IconSAP|Rabbit}} or {{IconSAP|Seal}}. Even without those two pets, Worm is a great scaling pet that can fit into most teams, providing convenient stat buffs, especially when levelled.

You can freeze Apples at the start of the turn to stockpile them for a big turn, such as waiting for a {{IconSAP|Bison}} on {{IconSAP|Tier 4}} or a Seal on {{IconSAP|Tier 5}}. You can freeze upwards of 4 Apples before the stocked food overtakes pet slots in the Shop.

In '''{{IconSAP|Custom Packs}}''', the Worm's Apple can be used to reliably trigger the {{IconSAP|Jerboa|name=Jerboa's}} ability, quickly scaling the whole team.
