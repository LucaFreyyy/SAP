The '''Apple''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} [[Food]], available in the '''{{IconSAP|Turtle Pack}}''', the '''{{IconSAP|Puppy Pack}}''' and the '''{{IconSAP|Golden Pack}}'''. When a pet is fed an Apple, it receives +1 attack and +1 health. The {{IconSAP|Pear}}, {{IconSAP|Lasagna}} and {{IconSAP|Chicken Leg}} are generally considered to be direct upgrades to it.

The {{IconSAP|Mouse}} and {{IconSAP|Worm}} will stock an Apple when sold/at the start of turn, at level 2 they will stock a {{IconSAP|Better Apple|nolink=yes}} and at level 3 they will stock a {{IconSAP|Best Apple|nolink=yes}} that give +2/+2 and +3/+3 respectively.
==Strategy==
Generally, the Apple is worth the 3 {{IconSAP|Gold}} cost during the early rounds since the stat boost is often what dictates a win, especially if it is being fed to a Pet that you plan to have stick around for a while and you do not want or need to buy many Pets from the shop. 

Can be stocked for free by a Mouse when sold and for 2 gold by a {{IconSAP|Worm}} at the start of turn.

If {{IconSAP|Jerboa}} is in the pack, it is beneficial for the Apples to be especially looked out for so the Jerboa can buff the rest of your Pets.

==Trivia==
*The Apple is one of only 3 foods to have the "Better" and "Best" upgrades to it, the others being {{IconSAP|Chocolate}} and {{IconSAP|Milk}}.
