'''Milk''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} [[Food]] that can only be obtained through the {{IconSAP|Cow}}'s ability. Depending on the level of the Cow, it can spawn '''Milk''' which gives +1/+2, '''{{IconSAP|Better Milk|nolink=yes}}''' which gives +2/+4, and finally '''{{IconSAP|Best Milk|nolink=yes}}''' which gives +3/+6.

== Strategy ==

==Trivia==
* The September 29th 2022 test build added a unique interaction when Cow was fed {{IconSAP|Chocolate}}, doing so would replace shop food with 2 free Chocolate Milk that gave +2 {{IconSAP|attack|nolink=yes}} and +1 {{IconSAP|health|nolink=yes}}. This interaction was removed in the next test build released the very next day.
