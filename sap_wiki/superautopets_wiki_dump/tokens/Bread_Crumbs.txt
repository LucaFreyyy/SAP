'''Bread Crumbs''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} [[Food]] that can only be obtained through the {{IconSAP|Pigeon}}'s ability. Selling the Pigeon stocks up to three (depending on the Pigeons level) free Bread Crumbs in the shop.

== Strategy ==
You can use the rabbit [https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/Rabbit] to let is be the scale value the same as the apple [https://superautopets.wiki.gg/wiki/Apple]

==Trivia==
*Bread Crumbs has the opposite effect of {{IconSAP|Seed Pile}} that is also obtainable by a {{IconSAP|Tooth Billed Pigeon|name=Tooth billed Pigeon}}.
