The '''Coconut''' is a {{IconSAP|Tier 7}} [[Food]], but isn't normally available in the shop and can only be obtained through abilities. The first time a pet with the Coconut perk takes damage, the damage is reduced to 0.

==Strategy==
Blocking all damage makes it a hard counter to {{IconSAP|Peanut}} and {{IconSAP|Peanut Butter}} perks, as well as allowing pets to survive high burst of damage regardless of their health. However it's not always better than the more readily available {{IconSAP|Melon}}, since Coconut cannot trigger Hurt abilities.

For example: When a 50/50 {{IconSAP|Gorilla}} with Coconut goes up against another 50/50 enemy with any defensive food perk, they will knock each other out after 2 hits. However if the Gorilla had had Melon or {{IconSAP|Garlic}} instead, it would've knocked the other 50/50 out in 2 hits, but gained Coconut after the first hit, survived, and could get a 3rd hit in against the second enemy.

==Obtaining Coconut outside battle==
Hurting a {{IconSAP|Gorilla}} in the shop phase will trigger its ability and give it permanent Coconut. This can be done by {{IconSAP|Sleeping Pill|name=Pilling}} a {{IconSAP|Hedgehog}} or {{IconSAP|Badger}}.

In Custom and Weekly packs, {{IconSAP|Coconut Crab}} can be used in the shop phase to gain a temporary Coconut. In order to give permanent Coconut to any pet or potentially the entire team, we'll need to create a Custom pack and follow these steps:

====Step 1: Parrot====
'''Goal''': At the end of turn, let a {{IconSAP|Parrot}} copy Gorilla's ability, then hurt the Parrot to trigger the copied ability and give itself Coconut.

This can be done by putting a {{IconSAP|Leech}} behind the Parrot, or with {{IconSAP|Deer Lord}} behind a Hedgehog or Badger. Remember that Parrot's copy ability has to go first, so make sure Leech/Deer Lord has lower attack than Parrot.

====Step 2: Transfer====
'''Goal''': At the end of turn, let the Parrot with Coconut copy a {{IconSAP|Seagull}}, faint a {{IconSAP|Chipmunk}} or {{IconSAP|Toucan}} or Seagull that has {{IconSAP|Mushroom}} perk, when that pet respawns, the Parrot will give it Coconut. Once Coconut is on one of these 3 pets, it can easily be copied to other pets on the team.

An example setup would be: Leech, Seagull with Mushroom, Parrot with Coconut, Seagull. The Seagull in front of the Parrot should not have any food perk, or has run out of triggers already.

Now, we need the Leech to knock out the Seagull in front. This can be guaranteed by putting that it at 1 health: by letting it get hurt by the Leech prior to this turn, feeding it {{IconSAP|Fried Shrimp}} or {{IconSAP|Eat-Me Cake}}, or giving it a Mushroom then Sleeping Pill so it will respawn as 1/1. Alternatively, putting {{IconSAP|Pineapple}} or {{IconSAP|Kiwano}} on the Leech will allow it to knock out a base stats Seagull immediately.

====Step 3: Chipmunk====
'''Goal''': Stock Coconuts in the shop with Chipmunk. Now we can fully pivot to any pets of our choosing and give them Coconut.

Congratulations! Hopefully that takes less than 20 turns, enjoy the full Coconut team!
