The Dirty Rat is a [[Tokens|Token]] that is summoned when {{IconSAP|Rat}} faints. Rather than joining the player's team, it will join the enemy team in the front-most space. If the enemy team is full, it will not be summoned. As with all tokens, it is considered a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} for the sake of certain abilities like {{IconSAP|Poodle|name=Poodle's}} and {{IconSAP|Rhino|name=Rhino's}}.
