The '''Ram''' is a [[Tokens|Token]] [[Pets|Pet]] with no abilities. Up to two of them spawn (depending on available slots) when a {{IconSAP|Sheep}} faints. As with all tokens, it is considered a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} for the sake of certain abilities like {{IconSAP|Poodle|name=Poodle's}} and {{IconSAP|Rhino|name=Rhino's}}. The Ram's starting stats increase depending on the level of the {{IconSAP|Sheep}} that summoned them.

== Strategy ==
An easy way to get the hidden sticker [[Achievements|Achievement]] for  the Ram is to level a Sheep to Level 2 and then use a [[Sleeping Pill]] on the Sheep while having an empty slot available. This will spawn two Level 2 Rams into the user's team which can then be combined into a single Level 3 Ram.  This is also particularly useful when trying to acquire a quick Level 3 Pet to activate the [[Bison|Bison's]] ability. As with all tokens, it is considered a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} for the sake of certain abilities like {{IconSAP|Poodle|name=Poodle's}} and {{IconSAP|Rhino|name=Rhino's}}.
