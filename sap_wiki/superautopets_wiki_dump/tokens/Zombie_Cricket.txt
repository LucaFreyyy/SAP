The '''Zombie Cricket''' is a [[Tokens|token]] which is summoned when a {{IconSAP|Cricket}} faints. Its level will be the same as the cricket it is summoned from. As with all tokens, it is considered a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} for the sake of certain abilities like {{IconSAP|Poodle|name=Poodle's}} and {{IconSAP|Rhino|name=Rhino's}}.
