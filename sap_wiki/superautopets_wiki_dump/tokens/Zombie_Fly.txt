The '''Zombie Fly''' is a [[Tokens|token]] that is summoned by the {{IconSAP|Fly}} when one of its friends faints. It is considered a {{IconSAP|Tier 1}} for the sake of certain abilities like {{IconSAP|Poodle|name=Poodle's}} and {{IconSAP|Rhino|name=Rhino's}}.

When a Zombie Fly faints, it will not trigger Fly's ability.
